# Zenithium: 2-minute demo video script

**Setup before recording:** TideCloak and the app are running. Both heirs have already set up their family keys. The vault is empty. Two browser windows are side by side: **left = Wei Jie (son)**, **right = Mei Ling (daughter)**, or a second laptop. Have a sample will PDF ready. Browser zoom is 110%; close other tabs.

| Time | Shot (screen) | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** · Problem | Home page: title and the 3 cards | "When a parent passes away in Malaysia, the will, the MyKad and the land titles usually sit with one person, one lawyer or one company. The family has to trust them blindly, and that's where disputes and fraud start. Zenithium removes that single point of trust." |
| **0:15–0:40** · Planner seals | Logged in as **Mr Lim Ah Kow** → *Seal the Vault*. Type the will summary, IC and assets; choose "Will (Wasiat)" and attach the PDF; click **Encrypt and seal the vault** → green "sealed" message | "Mr Lim writes down what his family will need and attaches his will. When he clicks *seal*, everything is encrypted **in his browser**, using TideCloak, before it leaves his laptop. The key that can open it is split between his two children, and Zenithium never gets it." |
| **0:40–1:00** · Database View | *Database View*: scroll the table (will, IC, assets and PDF are all ciphertext), then the raw JSON | "This is exactly what Zenithium's database holds, and what our admin, our hosting provider or a hacker would see: scrambled text. Only harmless labels like *document type* and *date* are readable. **We cannot read your family's will, even if we wanted to.**" |
| **1:00–1:20** · Son alone is blocked | Left window: **Wei Jie** → *Family Vault* → **Request unlock** → **Approve** → badge **1 OF 2 APPROVED** → **Open the vault** → warning "The vault stays sealed: 1 of 2…" | "Wei Jie wants to open it on his own. He requests and approves, but one approval isn't enough. Without his sister's key share, the vault key can't be rebuilt. This isn't a button we disabled; it's mathematically impossible." |
| **1:20–1:45** · Daughter approves, vault opens | Right window: **Mei Ling** logs in with her own Tide account → **Approve unlock**. Both windows: progress bar fills, badge **UNLOCKED**. **Open the vault** → will text appears → click the PDF, which opens in a new tab | "Mei Ling approves from her own Tide account on her own device. Now there are two of two approvals: the vault opens in their browsers, and the will PDF is readable, for the family only." |
| **1:45–2:00** · Why TideCloak + impact | Back to Home cards; optionally show a MOCKED.md / SIMULATED QUORUM badge flash | "In a normal app, the company holds the keys, so you have to trust the company. With TideCloak, keys are held by a decentralised network, and no single person, including us, can unlock alone. Zenithium protects widows, elderly spouses and families from disputes, supporting SDGs 16, 1 and 5. Our approval workflow is simulated in this prototype; the encryption is real." |

**Recording tips:** keep cursor movements slow; pre-type long text with a text expander or paste it; if the approval takes a moment, cut the wait in editing. Total ≈ 2:00.
